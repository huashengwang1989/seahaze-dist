# SEA Haze — Support

**English** · [华文](README.zh-Hans.md) · [Bahasa Melayu](README.ms.md) · [தமிழ்](README.ta.md)

SEA Haze shows haze and air quality across Singapore, Malaysia and Southeast Asia: live PM2.5, PSI and API readings, haze and wind on the map, satellite fire hotspots in Sumatra and Borneo, rain, and tropical cyclones.

Available for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch and Apple Vision Pro, with widgets. The app is in English, 华文, Bahasa Melayu and தமிழ்.

- **Privacy Policy:** [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md) — the app collects no personal data.
- **Contact and feedback:** [open an issue](../../issues) in this repository.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where does the data come from?**
Public sources: the National Environment Agency of Singapore (data.gov.sg), Malaysia's Department of Environment (APIMS), Open-Meteo (including Copernicus CAMS model data), Sensor.Community, NASA FIRMS, RainViewer and GDACS. You can also add your own free OpenAQ API key in Settings for more stations.

**Why do some readings show "≈"?**
"≈" marks a model estimate (Copernicus CAMS), shown only where there is no measuring station nearby. It is not a measurement.

**Why is some data missing?**
A data source may be temporarily unavailable. The app never shows invented or outdated values: data older than 30 minutes is hidden until a fresh update arrives. Tap Refresh at the top of the map, or wait for the next automatic update (every 15 minutes).

**How does "My Location" work?**
If you allow location access, the app picks the station nearest to you. Your location stays on your device. You can turn this off in Settings → Location.

**How do I add a widget?**
On iPhone or iPad, touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and search for "SEA Haze". On Mac, right-click the desktop and choose Edit Widgets. Edit the widget to choose a station or show Singapore's five regions.

**Can I see place names in my language?**
Yes. In Settings, set Location Names to App Language. The Place Names link at the bottom of Settings lists all translated names.
